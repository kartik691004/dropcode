# Mini Research Summary: Machine Learning

Title: Efficient Fine-Tuning with Low Compute Budget
Year: 2024

Abstract (Simplified):
This paper studies lightweight fine-tuning methods for transformer models under strict resource limits. It compares LoRA-style adaptation with full fine-tuning and demonstrates comparable performance in classification tasks with significantly lower compute usage.

Key Takeaways:
- Parameter-efficient methods reduce GPU memory cost.
- Good for student projects and rapid prototyping.
- Works well on domain-specific datasets.
